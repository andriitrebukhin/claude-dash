Ribbon

A desk stand for the Claude Dash display: one bent band, in the spirit of the Apple Studio Display stand. It prints without supports on a Bambu Lab A1 mini, all on one plate.

Download the plate (3MF) Fit test (STL) All files

In the box

  • Puck: holds the display
  • Bezel ring: black, press-fits over the glass edge
  • Stand: one bent band

You'll also need

  • 3 × 10 mm silicone bumpers for the foot
  • 4 × 6×2 mm magnets, 2 in the puck and 2 in the stand (optional; glue works too)
  • A USB-C cable, plug body up to 13 × 7.5 mm

Size

The screen sits about 8 cm up, leaning back 15°. The footprint is about 9 × 5 cm.

Puck Ø86 × 17.6 mm

Make it

  1. Check the fit (15 minutes)

    Print the fit test, the front slice of the puck. The display should drop in without force or wobble, and the ring should press in and stay. If not, adjust FIT, RING_FIT or MODULE_DEPTH in the model and rebuild.

  2. Print the plate

    Open the 3MF in Bambu Studio. Everything is already oriented, so no supports are needed. Give the ring black filament (with the AMS lite), or print it separately in black.

    PartSettings
    Puck0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid
    Stand0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill
    Ring0.12 mm layers, 100% infill, black

    Matte white or grey PLA looks the most like Apple aluminium. PETG works too.

  3. Assemble

    • Put the display into the puck from the front, with the USB-C ports at the bottom (they line up with the slots).
    • Press the ring in.
    • Fit the magnets (matching polarity) or glue the puck onto the stand. The slot on the back locates it.
    • Press the bumpers into the three recesses under the foot.
    • Plug the cable into the USB port: bottom right, as you look at the screen.

    BOOT and RESET are behind the two pinholes on the right edge; use a SIM tool.
